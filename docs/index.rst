Can You Get 24/7 Support?
=========================

Many users expect around-the-clock live support, but Voicenotes does not advertise a 24/7 live chat or phone support service on its official website.

Instead, the company provides:

* Email support
* Online Help Center
* Frequently Asked Questions
* Support resources within the application

The Help Center notes that response times may be slower during weekends and busy periods. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

Information to Include
======================

When emailing support, include:

* Registered account email
* Device model
* Operating system version
* App version
* Description of the issue
* Error messages
* Screenshots (if available)
* Billing receipt for payment-related requests

Providing complete details allows the support team to investigate your request more efficiently.

Tips for Faster Assistance
==========================

Before contacting support:

* Restart the application.
* Update to the latest version.
* Check your internet connection.
* Verify your account email.
* Try reproducing the issue.
* Gather screenshots or receipts if applicable.
